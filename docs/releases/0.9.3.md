# Macarok 0.9.3

Macarok 0.9.3 gives every list a softer selection and makes the timeline cursor move at a perfectly steady speed.

## Improvements

- **Softer selection everywhere**: library, playlist, podcast, radio, Internet Archive, and lyrics-search lists show their selection as a soft accent tint instead of the strong system band, so titles keep their normal colors. Adjacent selected rows merge into one rounded block, and the tint turns neutral while the window is inactive. Buttons, controls, and menus, including the source picker, keep the system accent.
- **Whole-album selection reads as one block**: a fully selected album in the playlist shows a stronger header band above a lighter body. Before, the header was narrower than its tracks, stayed blue while the tracks turned gray, and was drawn as a separate block.
- **A steady timeline cursor**: while audio plays, the cursor advances at exactly real-time speed, redrawn on every display frame; otherwise it stands still. Pausing and resuming never move it. Output latency, including a Bluetooth device renegotiating it, no longer changes its speed.

## Fixes

- **No jump when resuming**: the cursor used to freeze for about half a second after a resume, then jump forward, because VLC reports its position late after a resume. It now restarts from where it stopped as soon as playback resumes.
- **Library selection appears on click**: a clicked library row is highlighted immediately, instead of once the center panel has loaded.

## Download and install

Download `Macarok-0.9.3-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag Macarok to Applications. A SHA-256 checksum is provided as a separate release asset.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 14 or later. The application is signed with the Armateur Labs certificate, so privacy approvals carry over from 0.9.2. Keychain asks once per saved item (Last.fm, the YouTube API key, and each server) for your login keychain password after each update; choose **Always Allow**. It is not notarized by Apple: macOS blocks the first launch of a downloaded version. Approve it in **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Macarok.app` after copying the app.
