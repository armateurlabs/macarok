# Macarok 0.5.0

Macarok 0.5.0 adds the Internet Archive's Live Music Archive and LibriVox audiobooks as browsable sources, and fixes an interface freeze when changing tracks during network playback.

## Highlights

- **Live Music Archive**: add it from **Connect a Source…** and browse artists, then years and recordings with date, venue, recording source, and listener rating. Several recordings of the same show stay distinct. No account is needed.
- **LibriVox**: browse public-domain audiobooks by popularity, recent additions, or title, search titles and authors, and filter by language.
- **Browsed, not imported**: these collections load page by page as you browse and are cached on your Mac; earlier results remain visible when archive.org cannot be reached. Items join your library only when you play, queue, or save them. **Saved** and **Recent** tabs keep track of them, and saved items appear in Home and All Sources.
- **Quality**: **Settings › Playback › Internet Archive quality** streams lossless FLAC by default when a recording shares it, otherwise the best MP3. **Standard** uses smaller MP3 files. Stream-only recordings fall back automatically.
- **Audiobooks**: each book remembers the chapter and position where you stopped, with **Resume** and **Start Over**. Chapters or whole books can be downloaded for offline listening, alongside podcast downloads. Audiobooks are not scrobbled to Last.fm; live recordings are.
- **Responsiveness**: starting a new track no longer freezes the interface while libVLC closes the previous network connection, which could take close to a minute with some HTTP/2 servers.
- The library sort control uses chevrons, and Internet Archive navigation places **Back to Browse** before **Back** from the first level.

## Privacy

Browsing sends only your searches, filters, and the items you open to archive.org. Your library, listening history, and accounts are not sent. See [Internet Archive sources](https://github.com/armateurlabs/macarok/blob/v0.5.0/docs/SOURCES.md#internet-archive-live-music-archive-and-librivox).

## Download and install

Download `Macarok-0.5.0-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag Macarok to Applications. A SHA-256 checksum is provided as a separate release asset.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 14 or later. The application is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple; macOS may block the first launch.
