# Macarok 0.9.0

Macarok 0.9.0 adds a YouTube tab with the song's video, a native Share button, and a clearer Services settings tab.

## New

- **YouTube tab**: **Playing → YouTube** shows the song's video in YouTube's embedded player. Macarok uses a YouTube link known to MusicBrainz, then your own optional YouTube API key, then YouTube's search page in the tab, where choosing a video links it to the song. Videos are remembered for each song, and a video you choose always stays. Playing the video pauses your music, and playing music pauses the video. Videos whose owners refuse other apps offer **Watch on YouTube**.
- **Share**: a Share button at the end of the playback bar, **Share…** in the context menu of songs and episodes, a Share button in the menu bar mini player, and one in the YouTube tab open the native macOS share menu. A song is shared as `Title — Artist` with its Wikipedia article (the song's, otherwise the artist's) and YouTube video; a station with what it plays and its website; an episode with its page. Nothing mentions Macarok.
- **Services settings**: the Scrobbling tab becomes **Services**. Like Sources, it lists Last.fm and YouTube with their state and a **Configure…** button, and the services used without an account (Wikipedia, MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive, LRCLIB, ListenBrainz).

## Changes

- At the narrowest center panel width, the metadata tabs of Playing read **Edit song** and **Edit album** so all six tabs fit.

## Privacy

The artist and title are sent to MusicBrainz, Wikipedia, and YouTube only while the YouTube tab is shown or when you share a song. YouTube's cookies are kept in a store used only by Macarok's YouTube tab, separate from Safari. See [YouTube and sharing](../YOUTUBE.md).

## Keychain after updates

Macarok is signed with a personal certificate, not an Apple Developer ID. After each update, macOS asks once for your login keychain password for each saved item (Last.fm, the YouTube API key, and each server). Choose **Always Allow** so it is not asked again until the next update.

## Download and install

Download `Macarok-0.9.0-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag Macarok to Applications. A SHA-256 checksum is provided as a separate release asset.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 14 or later. The application is signed with the Armateur Labs certificate, so privacy approvals carry over from 0.8.0. It is not notarized by Apple: macOS blocks the first launch of a downloaded version. Approve it in **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Macarok.app` after copying the app.
