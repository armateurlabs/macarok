# Macarok 0.6.0

Macarok 0.6.0 gives podcasts their own library: shows at the first level, dated episodes sorted newest first, listening progress, chapters, and quiet automatic refresh.

## Highlights

- **Podcasts tab**: a new library tab, shown when the current source contains podcasts, with **Shows**, **New**, **In Progress**, and **Downloaded**. Shows open in place, with no folder to expand. In All Sources, podcasts no longer fill Artists, Albums, Recently Added, or Shuffle Library.
- **Episodes**: each row shows its publication date (Today, Yesterday, the weekday, then the date), an unplayed dot, the time left with a progress bar, and download or playback state. Filter a show by **All**, **Unplayed**, or **Downloaded**; the sort arrow switches between newest and oldest first.
- **Listening progress**: Macarok remembers where you stopped in each episode and resumes there. **Mark as Played**, **Mark as Unplayed**, **Mark All as Played**, and **Play from Beginning** are in the context menus.
- **Show Home**: an open show presents its artwork, description, **Continue Listening**, and **Latest Episodes**. All Sources Home adds **Continue Listening** and **New Episodes** across shows.
- **Chapters**: chapter starts appear as gaps on the timeline, with the chapter under the pointer on hover. The title row and the menu bar mini player show the current chapter, and chapter buttons, **Option-Left / Option-Right**, and **Playback › Previous / Next Chapter** move between chapters. Playing › Context lists them. Chapters come from Podcasting 2.0 files, Podlove chapters in the feed, or a chapter list in the episode notes.
- **Episode notes**: notes keep their paragraphs and lists, with clickable links and chapter times. Playing shows an episode's context directly, without the lyrics and metadata menu that do not apply to it.
- **Automatic refresh**: **Settings › Podcasts** checks shows every hour by default (or every 15 minutes, 6 hours, a day, or never), quietly and without interrupting a refresh you started. Unchanged feeds are not downloaded again, and a failed refresh is flagged on its show.
- **Library header**: search fields have a clear button (Escape also clears them); the library views form one grouped control, and the list heading shows its item count and sort direction.
- **Server scan**: **Library › Scan Library on Server** asks Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic servers to look for newly added files, follows the scan, then refreshes the source. A full scan is available too. Scans require an administrator account on the server; **Refresh Source** alone still only re-reads the server's existing catalog.
- Lyrics no longer show missing-glyph boxes where providers inserted invisible hyphenation characters.

## Upgrade notes

Feeds are fetched once more after the update to store show details, notes, and chapters. Episodes heard before this version count as played until marked unplayed.

## Download and install

Download `Macarok-0.6.0-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag Macarok to Applications. A SHA-256 checksum is provided as a separate release asset.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 14 or later. The application is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple; macOS may block the first launch.
